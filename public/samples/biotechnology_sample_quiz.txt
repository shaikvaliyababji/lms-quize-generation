What is the primary function of a bioreactor?
A. To store industrial chemicals
B. To cultivate microorganisms under controlled conditions
C. To separate solid wastes
D. To generate electricity directly
ANSWER: B

Which component of a bioreactor is responsible for introducing sterile air?
A. Impeller
B. Baffle
C. Sparger
D. Cooling jacket
ANSWER: C

The main function of baffles in a stirred-tank bioreactor is to:
A. Increase temperature
B. Prevent vortex formation
C. Measure dissolved oxygen
D. Control nutrient concentration
ANSWER: B

Which component provides mixing and improves oxygen transfer in a stirred-tank bioreactor?
A. Impeller
B. Thermometer
C. Sampling port
D. Gas outlet
ANSWER: A

Which material is commonly used for large-scale industrial bioreactors?
A. Wood
B. Plastic film
C. Stainless steel
D. Copper
ANSWER: C

What is the main purpose of a cooling jacket in a bioreactor?
A. To remove excess heat generated during fermentation
B. To increase oxygen solubility by adding chemicals
C. To measure biomass concentration
D. To sterilize incoming air
ANSWER: A

Which instrument is used to monitor dissolved oxygen concentration?
A. pH meter
B. Dissolved oxygen probe
C. Pressure gauge
D. Flow meter
ANSWER: B

Aerobic microorganisms require which of the following for growth and metabolic activity?
A. Methane
B. Nitrogen gas only
C. Oxygen
D. Hydrogen sulfide
ANSWER: C

Which of the following is an important application of aerobic bioreactors?
A. Methane production by anaerobic digestion
B. Penicillin production
C. Landfill methane recovery
D. Anaerobic solvent fermentation
ANSWER: B

Anaerobic bioreactors operate primarily under which condition?
A. High oxygen concentration
B. Absence of oxygen
C. Continuous air sparging
D. Excess dissolved oxygen
ANSWER: B
